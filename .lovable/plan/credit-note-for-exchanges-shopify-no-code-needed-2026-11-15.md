# Credit Note for exchanges (Shopify, no code needed)

Shopify can already do this. A "credit note" is issued as a **Shopify gift card code** of the exchange amount. The customer types it in the "Gift card / discount" box at checkout, which already works on your site.

## Owner's steps for each approved exchange

1. Customer emails or WhatsApps the owner (already on the site).
2. Owner checks the shirt and approves the exchange.
3. Owner works out the credit amount: product value minus shipping and handling charges (as your policy, section H, says).
4. In Shopify Admin: **Products → Gift cards → Issue gift card**.
   - Amount: the credit amount, e.g. 3,450
   - Code: let Shopify create it, or type one like `CN-1042` (order number) to match your records
   - Expiry: choose one (e.g. 1 year) or none
   - Customer: search for the customer's email so the card is linked to them
   - Internal note: "Credit note for order #1042, exchange approved [date]"
5. Click **Save**. Then on the gift card page use **Send to customer**. Shopify emails the code and balance.
6. The customer shops on the site. At checkout they enter the code and the amount comes off. Any leftover balance stays on the code for next time.

## Optional: link it to the original order

On the original order in Shopify, use **Return** to record the returned item. Then choose **Refund → Store credit / gift card** instead of money back. This keeps the exchange and the credit note tied to that order in your reports.

## Tracking

**Products → Gift cards** lists every credit note with its balance, who used it and when. You can turn off or change the expiry at any time.

## Why not "Store credit"

Shopify's built-in store credit only works when the customer is signed in to a Shopify customer account at checkout. Your site uses its own sign-in, so a gift-card code is more reliable for your customers.

## Website changes (optional, small)

- Add one sentence under section H of the exchange policy: "Your credit note will be emailed as a code to use at checkout." This is the only change. Nothing else in your policy wording changes.
