# Going live: Shopify is done, next is publish and GoDaddy

Your Shopify store is already claimed and on the Basic plan, so nothing more is needed to keep the store.

## Step 1 - Final Shopify checks (in Shopify admin)
- Add Razorpay (payments) and Shiprocket (shipping) through their Shopify apps if you haven't yet, then place one test order.
- Check every shirt has its photos, price, sizes and stock.

## Step 2 - Publish the website
- Click Publish, then check the live site on a phone and a laptop.

## Step 3 - GoDaddy
1. Log in, open My Products, then notionsinpattern.com, and check that the domain is active.
2. Turn off GoDaddy "Forwarding" and any GoDaddy website on this domain.
3. Leave the email records (MX, TXT) exactly as they are.
4. I open the domain connection here, and you sign in to GoDaddy on that screen. If that doesn't work, you add 2 "A" records and 2 "TXT" records by hand, using the values it shows.
5. Wait up to 72 hours. The secure padlock (https) is added automatically.

Use only notionsinpattern.com. Don't touch notioninpatterns.com.

## What I do once you approve
- Check how notionsinpattern.com can be connected, then open the connection card for you.
