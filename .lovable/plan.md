# Going live: Shopify first, then GoDaddy

Your site is already linked to your Shopify store, so products, prices and checkout already work. These steps make it live under notionsinpattern.com.

## Step 1 - Shopify (do this first)
1. Claim the Shopify store. This keeps the store and starts Shopify's 120-day free trial. A paid Shopify plan is needed after the trial to keep selling.
2. In Shopify admin, add Razorpay (payments) and Shiprocket (shipping) through their Shopify apps. Do a test order.
3. Check every product has its photos, price, sizes and stock.

## Step 2 - Publish the website
- Click Publish so the site goes live at its free address first. Check it on a phone and a laptop.

## Step 3 - GoDaddy (what to do there)
1. Log in to GoDaddy, open My Products, find notionsinpattern.com and check it is active and not expiring soon.
2. Open DNS for that domain. Don't delete anything yet. If you use email on this domain, leave those records (MX, TXT) exactly as they are.
3. Turn off GoDaddy "Forwarding" and any GoDaddy website builder site on this domain, because those would block the new site.
4. Then I start the domain connection from here. On the setup screen you sign in to GoDaddy and the records are added for you. If that doesn't work, you add 2 "A" records (@ and www) and 2 "TXT" records by hand, using the values shown on screen.
5. Wait. It usually works within a few hours, but it can take up to 72 hours. The secure padlock (https) is added automatically.

Use only notionsinpattern.com. Don't touch the similar notioninpatterns.com.

## Step 4 - After the domain works
- Set notionsinpattern.com as the main address.
- Set up email sender records so password-reset emails stop landing in spam.

## What I do once you approve
- Open the Shopify store-claim card for you.
- Open the GoDaddy domain connection for notionsinpattern.com once the site is published.
